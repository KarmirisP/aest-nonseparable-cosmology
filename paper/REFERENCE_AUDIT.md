# Reference audit

The final bibliography is designed for relevance rather than numerical padding. It includes modern AeST theory/phenomenology, Type-II/VCDM gravity, current cosmological data/likelihood methodology, and indispensable older foundations (MOND, TeVeS, CLASS, Planck, SPARC, Einstein-aether PPN). Recent entries were checked for journal, volume/article number, year, and DOI where available.

Particularly important modern anchors:
- Skordis & Zlosnik (2021, 2022): AeST construction and linear stability.
- Bataki, Skordis & Zlosnik (2024): fully nonlinear Hamiltonian formalism.
- Rosa & Zlosnik (2024), Verwayen et al. (2024), Durakovic & Skordis (2024), Reyes & Sakstein (2024/2025): cosmology, quasistatic phenomenology, cluster configurations, compact stars.
- De Felice et al. (2021, 2022), Ganz et al. (2023), Akarsu et al. (2024, 2026): VCDM / Type-II minimally modified gravity.
- Pantheon+, DESI, ACT, DES, Euclid-preparation and current Hubble-tension literature for observational context.

A final DOI resolver check is still recommended immediately before submission because journal metadata can be corrected after publication.
